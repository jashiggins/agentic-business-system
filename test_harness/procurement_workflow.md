Procurement Approval Workflow

1. Submit Request
   - Procurement Agent creates request with vendor, cost, business purpose, and attachments.

2. Budget Check
   - System checks budget bucket and policy:
     - < $500: auto-approve
     - $500–$5,000: Finance approval required
     - > $5,000: CEO + Procurement approval required

3. Approval Notification
   - Approvers receive templated request with one-click approve/deny.

4. Payment
   - On approval, Finance issues tokenized virtual card or payment method.

5. Reconciliation
   - Finance matches statements to receipts weekly; Audit reviews mismatches.

6. Vendor Risk
   - High-risk vendors require Security and Legal review before onboarding.
