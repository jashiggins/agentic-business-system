DATA_LEAK_RISK PLAYBOOK

Location: /bootstrap/incident_response_playbooks/data_leak_playbook.txt

Purpose
Detect, contain, and remediate suspected data exfiltration or bulk export of sensitive data.

Detection Criteria
- Large export or bulk download detected from storage or DB.
- Unusual access patterns to sensitive stores.
- Alerts from DLP or Privacy Agent.

Immediate Containment (first 0-30 minutes)
1. Revoke or suspend implicated accounts and service tokens.
2. Snapshot storage buckets and database states (read-only snapshots).
3. Enable enhanced logging and preserve all relevant logs.
4. Create immutable ledger entries for all preserved artifacts.

Forensics Checklist (first 0-24 hours)
- Identify scope of data accessed and exported.
- Collect access logs, query history, and agent actions.
- Hash and store evidence in immutable ledger.

Communications
- Notify CEO, Security, Privacy, Legal, and Finance.
- Prepare customer and regulator notifications per jurisdiction.

Legal/Regulatory Triggers
- If regulated PII or financial records are exposed, trigger legal notification workflows.

Remediation Steps (24–72 hours)
1. Revoke temporary credentials and rotate keys.
2. Block exfiltration endpoints and tighten egress rules.
3. Reassess and tighten least-privilege policies and DLP rules.

Post‑Incident Review
- Update access controls, perform privacy impact assessment, and schedule audits.
